# Bundled admin alarm sound

`app/src/main/res/raw/admin_alert.ogg` and the web copies at `public/sounds/admin_alarm.ogg` and `public/sounds/admin_alarm.wav` are derived from **Sirens and Alarm Noise** by **aquinn**, published on [OpenGameArt](https://opengameart.org/content/sirens-and-alarm-noise) under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). The source file is `siren.mp3` from that page. Attribution is optional under CC0 but retained here for provenance.

The bundled version is a six-second excerpt (source time 22–28 seconds), converted to mono, normalized to a 0.88 sample peak, and given 15 ms edge fades. Its decoded 44.1 kHz audio measures approximately 0.32 RMS, versus 0.14 RMS for the previous 1.7-second clip. Perceived loudness depends on the device and notification-volume setting.
