Kwik-Parsel privacy policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Kwik-Parsel is a product of Gohezoh Integrated Services Ltd, No. 13A Orji Woko Close, off Police Post Road, Eliozu, Port Harcourt, Rivers State, Nigeria. Questions or requests about your information can be sent to info@gohezohservices.org.

Information we use

Accounts use your name, email, phone number and role. Deliveries use pickup and destination addresses, coordinates, sender and recipient contact details, parcel descriptions and photos, status updates, and payment and payout records. Companies may submit registration documents, owner NIN and face photo, rider NIN and licence evidence, vehicle papers, and bank details. We collect the location of riders who enable tracking. We use these details to provide deliveries, confirm identity and handovers, calculate routes, pay partners, prevent misuse, and meet legal obligations.

Rider location

The Android rider app collects location data to enable dispatch, customer delivery tracking, company vehicle and rider safety, and authorized location requests even when the app is closed or not in use. While on duty, it updates location automatically. While off duty, it checks about once an hour for an authorized request and sends a fresh location only when requested. An Android notification is visible while the tracking service runs. A company can view its rider's location; a customer can request a rider's location only for a paid active delivery, subject to the app's rate limit. A last-known position may be stale. Riders can manage location permission in Android settings. See the rider location terms.

Who receives information

We use Supabase for app accounts, database, files and server functions. Payment or payout details may be processed by Paystack or Flutterwave depending on the transaction. Address and route searches may use Google Maps or Geoapify. Delivery-code messages, when enabled, may use an SMS or WhatsApp provider. The assigned logistics company and rider receive only the delivery details needed for the job; customers receive limited rider and company details for their delivery. Super Admin reviewers can access identity evidence. We do not sell personal information.

Security and retention

Identity evidence is kept in private storage, subject to access controls. We retain account and delivery information while needed to run the service, handle disputes, prevent fraud and meet accounting or legal obligations. Precise rider fixes used for live tracking are kept as recent or last-known positions rather than a full route history; see the rider terms for the 24-hour company-visible last-known limit. When an account is deleted, records no longer needed for those purposes should be removed or de-identified. Some financial or delivery records may need to be retained; we will explain these in a deletion response.

Your choices and rights

You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information, object to processing where applicable, or contact us with a privacy complaint. You can request account deletion here from a browser without reinstalling the app; sign in to prove which account is yours. You can also email us. Active deliveries or unsettled payouts may need to be resolved before account deletion. You can withdraw device location permission in Android settings, though rider dispatch and tracking will then stop working.
