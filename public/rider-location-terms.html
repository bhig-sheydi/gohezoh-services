Rider location terms

When you enable location sharing in the Kwik-Parsel Android app, your phone reports its position to support dispatch, delivery tracking, and the safety of riders, parcels, and company vehicles. A visible notification remains while the tracking service runs. Going off duty pauses new job matching but keeps the phone available for authorized location requests.

While on duty, the phone reports position automatically. While off duty, it checks for requests about once an hour and sends a fresh position only when an authorized request is pending. Your logistics company can request your position and view a clearly dated last-known position. Customers can request it only for their paid, active deliveries, at most twice in a rolling hour. Customer access ends when the delivery is completed or cancelled.

If you grant Android "Allow all the time" background-location access, the app can try to resume its visible service after a restart. Android may still stop it because of battery settings, a force-stop, revoked permission, disabled GPS, no internet, or an expired tracking grant. Last-known positions must not be treated as live.

You can stop sharing through the Android notification or phone permission controls. Signing out or revocation of rider access also ends the grant. Company access is limited to authorized managers and staff for legitimate delivery, vehicle, and rider-safety purposes. Precise location is stored as a recent fix rather than a travel history; company-visible last-known fixes expire after 24 hours. Contact your company management about location-access concerns.
