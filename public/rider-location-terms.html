Rider location terms

When you turn on phone location sharing in the Kwik-Parsel Android app, your phone reports its current position to support dispatch, delivery tracking, and the safety of riders, parcels, and company vehicles. A visible notification remains while the tracking service runs. Going off duty pauses new job matching but does not stop sharing.

Your logistics company can view your current position and a clearly dated last-known position. A customer can check your position only for their paid, active delivery, at most twice in a rolling hour. Customer access ends when that delivery is completed or cancelled. Location access is limited to these roles and purposes.

If you grant Android “Allow all the time” background-location access, the app can try to resume sharing after a phone restart. If you grant only while-in-use access, tracking may continue after you lock the screen once started, but it cannot reliably restart after a reboot. Android may still stop it because of battery settings, a force-stop, revoked permission, disabled GPS, no internet, or an expired tracking grant. Last-known positions must not be treated as live.

You can stop sharing from the rider screen or the persistent notification. Signing out or revocation of rider access also ends the grant. A company should use location for legitimate delivery, vehicle, and rider-safety purposes, with access restricted to authorized managers and staff. Precise location is stored as a recent fix rather than a travel history; old company-visible fixes expire after 24 hours. Contact your company management about location-access concerns.
