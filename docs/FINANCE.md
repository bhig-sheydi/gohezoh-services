# Finance phase

Finance, Management, and Admin users can maintain per-job partner cost and other direct costs. Customer revenue and currency are read from the confirmed order; the database freezes those values once a delivery job exists so customer balances and finance limits stay aligned. The dashboard groups full-ledger totals by currency and derives outstanding customer balance, gross profit, and unpaid partner settlements. The detail list shows the most recent 100 jobs.

Finance can record customer receipts against a job and create a partner settlement only for a delivered or closed job with an assigned partner. Every customer receipt requires a reference unique per job, and every settlement requires a unique request reference per job. The payout has a separate bank transfer reference that must be entered before marking the settlement paid. Reusing a reference fails instead of adding another ledger entry. Management or Admin approves settlements; the creator cannot approve their own settlement. Finance, Management, or Admin records the payout, and the approver cannot record that payout. Total received customer receipts cannot exceed recorded revenue, and active partner settlements cannot exceed the recorded partner cost. Mistaken receipts can be reversed with a reason; the record and audit history remain, while balances and totals count only received receipts. Customers see a safe projection of their own receipt history, including reversal status, plus billed amount and remaining balance; internal receipt notes/references, costs, and partner settlements remain private.

This is a manual reconciliation ledger. It does not initiate card, bank, POS, or online payments, and it does not store bank account or card credentials. All financial writes go through role-checked database functions and leave audit records. See the Finance migration and SQL integration test for the enforced rules.
