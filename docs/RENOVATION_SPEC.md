# Kwik-Parsel Core Logistics Renovation Specification\n\n**Status:** Current working product specification, updated 2026-10-13.\n**Source:** `Gohezoh_Software_Review_and_Corrective_Developer_Specification (1).pdf` plus the latest explicit pricing instruction. This file is the current, ordered requirements reference; the source PDF remains unchanged.\n\n## Policy change: driver transport fee\n\nThe latest instruction changes the transport pricing model in the source PDF. For every completed, eligible delivery leg performed by a driver, rider, or transport partner, Gohezo's platform fee is **20% of the partner's accepted, collected charge**. The fee is withheld from the partner's settlement; it is not added as a separate 20% charge to the customer's price.\n\n- The partner enters a customer-facing bid with the 20% deduction disclosed, and is responsible for pricing with that deduction in mind.\n- If the accepted and collected charge for a leg is NGN 100,000, the platform fee is NGN 20,000 and the partner's payable amount is NGN 80,000, before any separately agreed adjustments.\n- The customer sees and approves the accepted quote and any separately applicable, disclosed service fees. The system must not add another 20% platform fee to the customer quote.\n- For a job with multiple driver/transport legs, calculate the fee on each eligible leg's own accepted and collected charge. Do not calculate 20% repeatedly against the whole-job amount.\n- Do not accrue the fee for an unaccepted quote, an unperformed/cancelled leg, or an uncollected amount. Apply refunds and corrections as linked ledger adjustments; preserve the original records.\n- Show the partner the gross accepted charge, the 20% fee, adjustments, and net payable before award acceptance and on settlement records. Preserve a versioned, auditable record of the policy used.\n- This fee is separate from BDO/marketer referral commissions, which have their own approved agreements and ledger rules. It is also separate from warehouse, packaging, and other explicitly accepted service fees.\n\nFor driver transportation, this policy **supersedes** the source PDF's `total eligible direct cost / 0.75` customer-price formula and its 25%-contribution pricing example. Do not stack that formula on top of the 20% partner deduction. The partner's accepted quote sets the customer-facing transport charge; Operations may not silently add an unquoted margin or fee. Any separately billed service or direct cost must be disclosed in the quote and accepted by the customer before commitment. The superseded formula is not a rule for warehouse service pricing.\n\n## Ordered core features\n\n### P0 — Make transport bookable and commercially clear\n\n1. **Approved service areas and corridors.** Provide local/intra-state and interstate service choices. Initially enable the specified Lagos–Port Harcourt, Lagos–Aba, and Port Harcourt–Aba directions. Keep Umuahia available only on explicitly configured route pairs. Filter destination choices from the selected origin. Reject unsupported routes in the backend. Management can activate or retire corridors with an audit record; existing accepted jobs are not silently cancelled.\n2. **Fast, structured shipment request.** Capture origin and destination addresses, parcel type, dimensions/weight where known, quantity, timing, and handling requirements. Validate required information, show serviceability early, and issue a human-readable request reference.\n3. **Eligible partner discovery and private bidding.** Invite only approved partners matching the exact corridor, service, and known capacity. Start the default two-hour quote window when the request is published. Store server-side open/deadline/close times. Partners submit amount, availability, delivery estimate, inclusions, exclusions, and declared surcharge conditions. A partner cannot see competing bids or bidder identities.\n4. **Automatic, auditable bid decision.** At the deadline, exclude late or invalid bids and select the lowest valid quote. Retain all bids and disqualification reasons. Route no-bid and tied-lowest outcomes to the configured tie-break or Operations review; never invent a price. If the winner declines or misses confirmation, offer the next valid quote if still valid or escalate.\n5. **Customer quote and approval.** Present the selected partner's customer-facing transport quote, disclosed fees/conditions, validity, and delivery estimate. The quote must reflect the partner's bid, with no extra 20% Gohezo fee added to the customer. Any change to route, address, parcel, service speed, or handling requires re-quotation and customer re-approval.\n6. **Partner-side 20% fee and net payout preview.** Before the partner bids and accepts an award, explain that 20% of the accepted, collected eligible transport charge is withheld from the partner settlement. Show gross charge, fee, and net payable. Apply the fee once per completed transport leg and make its calculation reproducible in the ledger. Do not confuse this fee with referral commissions.\n7. **Reliable acceptance and award.** Confirm a job only after customer acceptance and required payment or approved credit terms. Give the winning partner a configurable confirmation period. Keep quote, acceptance, payment/credit, award, and partner-confirmation events linked and auditable.\n\n### P0 — Deliver safely and prove custody\n\n8. **Clear status and tracking timeline.** Give the customer a reference and readable progress updates. Record pickup, each custody handoff, transit, hub arrival, last-mile dispatch, delivery verification, and proof. Notify customers at meaningful state changes without claiming an event before it is verified.\n9. **Port Harcourt inbound hub handling.** Any shipment destined for Port Harcourt requires Gohezo hub receipt and last-mile handling. Record parcel scan, condition, custody transfer, and receipt time, then create/link the onward last-mile leg. A direct-delivery bypass requires an authorized, reasoned, audited exception. The source specification's NGN 500 is not a blanket fee; charge it only if the selected, approved service terms say it applies.\n10. **Recipient OTP and proof of delivery.** For OTP-required jobs, issue a random, expiring, single-use code to the recipient at the delivery stage. The recipient gives it to the delivery partner, who enters it; only backend verification permits completion. Log attempts and resends without readable OTPs in logs. A photo alone cannot satisfy OTP. Unavailable/invalid OTPs use an authorized exception path. Record and expose permitted proof of delivery.\n11. **Exceptions, cancellation, and recovery.** Handle no partner, quote expiry, partner decline, delayed pickup, route change, failed handoff, OTP failure, damage, and cancellation explicitly. Stop pending quote work when a request expires or is cancelled. Preserve custody and financial history; re-quote and obtain approval where service terms materially change.\n\n### P1 — Improve partner value and fulfillment\n\n12. **Partner work and earnings workspace.** Show eligible opportunities, quote countdowns, required shipment details, own bid/award state, assigned work, route/custody actions, OTP entry, proof submission, and the partner's own fee and settlement breakdown. Never expose another partner's data, competing bids, or Gohezo's internal referral network.\n13. **Payment and settlement traceability.** Link customer collected amounts, refunds, each partner leg's accepted charge, 20% fee, partner net payable, and actual settlement references. Block duplicate receipts/payouts and unauthorized approvals. Do not treat a browser redirect or payment claim as verified collection.\n14. **Gohezo-owned warehouse fulfillment.** Support customer inventory intake, condition and location records, stock movement, order reservation, picking, packing, and dispatch linked to a delivery job. Show processing, storage, packaging materials, and transport separately. The source PDF's NGN 1,000 processing and NGN 2,000 standard-carton/month figures are proposed reference rates; five-item limits, returns charges, and partial-month rules require approval before billing.\n15. **Partner growth and service coverage.** Make partner value concrete through clear service-area requirements, capacity/availability, fair confidential bidding, predictable award rules, and transparent net earnings and settlements. Add new corridors, vehicle/service classes, size/weight tiers, and services through configuration rather than code redesign.\n\n## Decisions still requiring configuration or approval\n\n- Exact additional origin/destination pairs for Umuahia.\n- Whether urgent local jobs use a shorter quote window than the default two hours.\n- Approved quote rounding increments, if any; do not silently alter partner bids.\n- Full pre-award payment versus approved credit terms for selected customer accounts.\n- The eligible charge base for unusual cases such as split/partial collections, refunds, cancelled legs, or a shipment with non-driver service fees. The implementation must keep these out of the customer-facing 20% fee and represent them transparently in the settlement ledger.\n- Whether OTP is required on all deliveries or only configured delivery classes; the Port Harcourt final leg remains OTP-required under the current workflow.\n- Warehouse billing scope and rates listed above.\n\n## Acceptance checks\n\n1. Disabled corridors are hidden in customer selection and rejected server-side; enabling one updates options and writes an audit record.\n2. Only eligible, approved partners receive a request; partner bids remain confidential and the window closes at its server deadline.\n3. The lowest valid bid wins; late/invalid bids, no bids, ties, and winner decline follow explicit audited paths.\n4. The customer quote equals the accepted customer-facing bid plus only separately disclosed and accepted service fees. No additional 20% platform fee is added to the customer.\n5. For an eligible completed leg collected at NGN 100,000, the ledger shows NGN 20,000 platform fee and NGN 80,000 partner net before separately agreed adjustments. The same job cannot accrue the fee twice for one leg.\n6. Multiple transport legs calculate the fee only on each leg's own eligible amount; no whole-job duplicate fee is created.\n7. Customer changes to route or parcel terms force a revised quote and renewed acceptance.\n8. Port Harcourt inbound work cannot skip hub receipt and linked last-mile handling without authorized, audited override.\n9. A wrong, expired, or missing required OTP blocks delivery completion; a valid code allows it and records verification and proof.\n10. Customer receipts, refunds, partner fees, partner net payables, and settlement transfers reconcile without duplicate entries.\n11. Warehouse fees are displayed and accepted separately and do not silently inherit the driver transport fee.\n\n## Source relationship\n\nThis specification reorganizes the logistics features from the corrective PDF into implementation order. The PDF's coverage, confidential bidding, Port Harcourt hub, OTP, warehouse, partner, and dashboard requirements remain guidance except where revised here. The explicit 20% partner-side transport fee and replacement customer-price rule above supersede the PDF's driver-transport price formula. BDO/marketer commissions remain governed by their separately approved agreement and do not alter this platform fee.\n\n## Change history\n\n- **2026-10-13:** Replaced the driver-transport customer-price formula with a 20% platform fee withheld from partner settlement; updated the ordered transport scope and settlement acceptance checks.
