# Kwik-Parsel Core Features

This is the ordered customer-and-partner product scope for local and interstate goods transportation. Detailed requirements and acceptance checks are in [RENOVATION_SPEC.md](RENOVATION_SPEC.md). For new local jobs, Gohezo retains 25% of the selected, collected customer charge and records 75% as the company's delivery fee; it does not add a separate fee to the customer's quote. Historical jobs keep their recorded terms.

## Build order

**Partner review experience:** Super Admin approves a direct company application without choosing an existing company; approval creates its company record atomically. Invitation and BDO-referral linking remains intact. Group company information, business documents, vehicle papers and driver details in one application card. Preview private documents on the same page in a modal with Cancel at the top. Completed onboarding steps appear gray with Done and Edit controls. Rejecting an application requires a reason, stored on the application and delivered to the applicant in the access screen and an in-app notification. Existing email, ownership, fleet and licence checks still apply.

**Partner form layout:** Use a wider onboarding card (up to 960 px) with two-column vehicle fields and fleet cards on larger screens. Keep phone layouts in one readable column and shorten repeated instructions.

**Partner onboarding presentation:** Use a horizontal Documents → Vehicles → Review slide flow, showing one step at a time. Back/Next preserve unfinished input. Merchants can save several vehicles, see their fleet list, and choose Add another vehicle before continuing. Require a saved business document before Vehicles and at least one saved vehicle before Review. Save an unfinished vehicle before entering Review; honour reduced-motion preferences.

**Partner company onboarding (2026-10-15):** Local delivery riders must work under a registered logistics company; independent riders join a company rather than registering a personal vehicle as a company fleet. After business verification documents, company applicants register at least one vehicle: motorcycle, tricycle, car, van, bus or truck. Capture company-name registration papers, plate number, driver full name, 11-digit NIN, driver licence and daily opening/closing times in Nigeria time. These motor-vehicle categories require a licence; do not offer an unverified exemption. Super Admin privately reviews ownership and driver evidence before granting access. Matching names and NIN format do not constitute government verification. Preserve company invitations and BDO attribution. Existing approved companies are not retroactively disabled by this onboarding change. Company staff joining through a verified invitation do not register another company fleet.

1. **Route availability:** Customer selects local or interstate service, then only enabled origin/destination corridors. Backend rejects disabled routes. Umuahia route pairs are explicit.
2. **Shipment request:** Collect pickup/drop-off details, parcel type, known dimensions and weight, quantity, timing, and handling needs; return a request reference.
2a. **Customer saved parcels and focused workspaces:** Let standalone customer accounts save photographed, frequently sent items with declared worth, price paid, dimensions, and weight; choose one to prefill a request and select 1–50 matching items for one destination. Warn as soon as the selected count exceeds the estimated capacity, combining item weight and a rotatable regular-grid size estimate. Keep Parcel Pals prominent, and open Send items, Updates, and Tracking as separate customer app frames. Enforce a packed shipment cap of 99 × 60 × 70.5 cm and 9.9 kg (three times the researched 33 × 20 × 23.5 cm, 3.3 kg reference carton) until carrier-specific tiers are defined.
2b. **Parcel Pal delivery destinations:** Customer accounts may save up to three destinations (Work, Home, Other), each with address, city, state, an optional alternate phone, and, when confirmed, coordinates. Open the editor from **Edit my delivery info**. Explicit device geolocation is optional when physically present; server-backed Geoapify autocomplete offers Nigeria-filtered addresses. A labeled tiled map lets customers drag or tap to correct a pin on mobile without reloading the whole map after each move. A nearby street-name autocomplete with a wider Nigeria lookup when the local map is wrong, and a search field for mapped side streets and closes around the chosen street, plus landmarks whose mapped street matches it, help narrow broad results. Common informal descriptions such as "school gate" retry by the mapped place type when the full phrase has no result. Customers must confirm a side street actually connects to the chosen street; proximity alone does not prove road connectivity; neither source claims every street, landmark, or building is mapped. Broad or low-confidence geocodes require pin correction before a new shipment is confirmed. Keep written addresses editable, and require customer confirmation of pickup and delivery points. Store coordinates, source, device-reported accuracy where applicable, and timestamp on the request; never present an address-geocoder point as a GPS fix. No IP-location fallback. Only accepted Parcel Pals can see saved address details. See [Location detection](LOCATION_DETECTION.md) for API and privacy details.
3. **Partner matching:** Invite only approved partners eligible for the corridor, service, and capacity.
4. **Private one-minute company bidding:** Invited companies submit their price, availability, estimated timing, inclusions, exclusions, and declared surcharges. Partners cannot see competing bids. Show the customer an animated search and a timed progress indicator while bids are open.
5. **Customer company choice and rider routing:** After the deadline, show every valid company bid, company name and phone, and the invited rider's name, photo, and phone when supplied. Clearly label missing rider details. The customer chooses one company; no automatic lowest-bid winner or tie decision applies to this flow. Route a pending pickup notice to the rider who sourced that company's invitation, without releasing customer contact details or permitting pickup yet. When the paid award is accepted, assign that rider to the real job if still approved and below the six-active-parcel limit; otherwise resolve the assignment within the selected company. Keep bid records and no-bid retry paths. Remind the customer to compare the arriving rider's face with the profile photo before handing over goods.
6. **Local delivery price and payment:** The selected company bid is the final customer charge for local delivery. Show a two-minute NGN temporary account immediately after selection. The rider sees a waiting task but may not move until provider-verified payment creates the confirmed job. Expire the rider hold after two minutes; send any late confirmed transfer to manual review. Interstate pricing keeps its existing Management approval flow until explicitly changed. Requote if shipment details change.
7. **Partner fee preview:** Disclose that Gohezo retains 25% of the accepted and collected local transport charge and records 75% as the company's delivery fee. Show customer charge, platform share, and company share before award acceptance. Do not charge on unselected bids.
8. **Customer acceptance and confirmed award:** For local delivery, selecting the company and paying the exact bid within the hold confirms the award automatically. For other services, keep required quote acceptance and payment or approved credit before award. Handle declined and expired awards.
9. **Shipment tracking and custody:** Show accurate customer milestones and record partner handoffs, scans, condition, and timestamps. After confirmed payment, the customer can open their active delivery's rider tracking view. It refreshes recent speed, straight-line distance and a clearly approximate ETA each minute while open, without exposing precise coordinates. The customer may reveal one current location snapshot, mapped community, street and closest mapped landmark, plus its map, at most twice per rolling hour across their account. The server enforces this limit; unavailable GPS does not consume a check. Stale positions disappear after two minutes; tracking ends on delivery completion or cancellation. The rider's browser must remain open for updates, and no route history is retained.
10. **Port Harcourt inbound flow:** Require Gohezo hub receipt and linked last-mile delivery for Port Harcourt-bound shipments, subject only to an audited authorized exception.
11. **Verified delivery:** The recipient OTP remains the normal server-verified completion method. If the recipient cannot provide it, the assigned rider may request confirmation from the authenticated booking customer. That customer can approve only after independently verifying the recipient received the parcel, or reject and leave it in transit. A rider's button, call, or photo alone cannot complete delivery.
12. **Exceptions and financial reconciliation:** Handle delays, failed handoffs, cancellation, refunds, and quote changes. Paid but undelivered company earnings remain pending. After OTP or booking-customer confirmation completes delivery, the recorded company fee becomes available. A company manager selects a supported Nigerian bank, enters a ten-digit account number, sees the provider-resolved account name, and explicitly confirms that payout destination. Bank verification may use a separate `FLUTTERWAVE_ACCOUNT_VERIFY_SECRET_KEY`; a live verification key checks real accounts while the transfer key remains in test mode. Without it, Flutterwave sandbox verifies only Access Bank sample accounts 0690000031–0690000041. In the mixed-key setup, withdrawals remain disabled. Withdrawals reserve the payable once and initiate one automatic Flutterwave NGN bank transfer. A queued transfer is not paid; only a separately verified successful provider result marks it paid. A confirmed failure releases the reservation. A timeout or uncertain result stays reserved for reconciliation and must never trigger a second transfer automatically. Sandbox transfers are visibly labelled as tests. Live transfer initiation stays disabled unless the server setting `FLUTTERWAVE_LIVE_PAYOUTS_ENABLED=true` is added after Flutterwave's KYC, funding, transfer permission, webhook, and outbound-IP requirements have been verified.
13. **Partner workspace:** Give partners their own opportunities, bids, assignments, delivery actions, fee calculations, and settlement status while keeping other partners' data private.
14. **Gohezo fulfillment add-on:** Add owned-warehouse inventory, storage, processing, picking, packing, and dispatch after the transportation flow is reliable; quote and accept applicable fees separately.
15. **Expand by configuration:** Add corridors, service classes, partner capacity options, and parcel size/weight tiers without redesigning the core booking flow.

## Commercial rule

The minimum partner bid and customer-facing charge for any local or interstate dispatch is NGN 1,000. A delivery cannot be confirmed below this amount.

For a new local job with NGN 100,000 selected and collected, Gohezo's share is NGN 25,000 and the company delivery fee is NGN 75,000. The company sets a customer-facing bid knowing that split; no extra customer fee is added. An unselected bid earns neither party money. Earlier jobs retain their recorded 80% company fee. This is distinct from BDO/marketer referral commissions and warehouse fees. The prior `eligible direct cost / 0.75` price formula does not apply to local driver transport under this policy.

## Release gates

- **P0:** Features 1–12; server-side coverage, bid selection, quote acceptance, fee ledger, hub/custody rules, OTP, and financial reconciliation must pass their acceptance tests.
- **P1:** Features 13–14; partner self-service and Gohezo-owned warehouse fulfillment.
- **P2:** Feature 15; coverage and service expansion after the configured launch corridors operate reliably.

**Company dashboard identity (2026-10-15, updated 2026-10-22):** Company managers see a greeting based on the device's local hour, the registered company contact name, an editable private company logo, and today's NGN earnings from fully paid jobs confirmed delivered during the device's local calendar day. The dashboard separately shows available company fees, paid deliveries awaiting completion, and automatic withdrawal status. Other partner staff cannot change the logo or view this company-level earnings card.

**Company bidding screen (2026-10-15):** Put a dark, television-style live bidding panel directly beneath the company welcome card. Show eligible open requests with the saved parcel's photo or a customer-uploaded request photo when available, per-item weight and dimensions, item count, pickup and delivery cities, a blue Bid action, and a countdown to the server deadline. New and reopened quote windows last one minute; the server rejects late bids. Keep the panel empty when no eligible job exists. A rider is assigned after the winning bid and job award, so no rider name or live position is claimed during bidding. A goods photo is required and each sent item is saved to the customer catalogue. A company sees a pickup only while it has an eligible invited rider.
**Live partner bidding (2026-10-15):** Subscribe the company bidding panel to row-level-secured quote-window changes so eligible orders appear without a browser refresh. Refresh on reconnect/focus and use a five-second visible-tab fallback poll. The countdown remains based on the server deadline; expired jobs are hidden immediately.
**Company riders (2026-10-15):** Drivers already submitted with an approved company vehicle become that company's rider roster automatically, without another name entry. A new rider needs an 11-digit NIN, NIN document and driving licence; Super Admin approves the submission before the company can issue login credentials. Company managers can add a photo, generate a memorable unique password or set a unique custom password, and share one company `/rider` link by copy or WhatsApp. Each rider has a separate internal identity without a personal email and sees only individually assigned deliveries. The first successful login binds a browser-held, non-exportable signing key; the password continues to work on that phone, but not another. Four valid-password attempts from another phone disable the password and alert management to create a replacement. Management can regenerate the password and phone binding or remove the rider immediately; the rider record remains archived. The dashboard shows the browser-reported phone model when available, which is not a verified hardware identity. Browser apps cannot read a phone MAC address. Riders never receive owner or general company-management access.

**Automated local dispatch (2026-10-16, updated 2026-10-22):** A customer chooses a saved pickup address with coordinates, an address suggestion, or an explicitly confirmed lookup, and may correct the pickup pin. Both local and interstate deliveries require a confirmed pickup and destination point. Device GPS is optional when physically at pickup; address-search coordinates are labelled approximate. The rider goes on duty explicitly in the browser, which sends fresh readings only while visible; a stored current fix expires from routing after two minutes. A spatial query considers approved, available riders within 5 km who have spare pickup capacity. It creates one private, one-minute bid invitation per company, not per rider. Companies bid; the customer sees their raw bids after the window closes and selects a company. The invited nearby rider sees a pending pickup immediately; after the paid award is accepted, the same rider gets the real job if still eligible, otherwise company dispatch assigns another approved rider. Without a qualifying rider, the database queue retries every two minutes until a suitable rider appears or the request is cancelled. The one-minute bid window starts only after a rider is found. A company bid window that closes without bids remains a separate customer retry case. Operations has no access to these automated pickup requests. Only invited company managers see bidding pickup and delivery pins; assigned riders see the delivery pin after award. No Google Maps or AI service is needed for matching; Geoapify is called for explicit address lookup, optional street or landmark search, nearby mapped places, and the compact correction map. This routing mechanism controls infrastructure cost but does not by itself prove commercial profitability; delivery pricing, partner fees, and payout reconciliation must satisfy the commercial rule above.

**Route priority and six-parcel rule (2026-10-22):** The current local matcher road-checks up to three nearby riders and three farther riders whose committed next stop is near pickup. Invite a feasible nearby rider whose existing route fits first; if none qualifies, invite a feasible farther rider on a matching route; only then fall back to another eligible nearby rider. The one-minute bidding window starts after matching. Ordinary delay to parcels already assigned to the rider is not a matching priority or score penalty; the new pickup ETA limit still applies. Each rider may have at most six assigned, unfinished parcels at once, counting parcels already in transit. A seventh cannot be invited or assigned. The rider may mark their box Full sooner because parcel count cannot measure physical fit. A road-provider failure leaves the request in the persistent search because closing-time feasibility cannot be verified without a road estimate; a bounded candidate search does not guarantee the globally best rider.

**Fuel-first stop sequencing (2026-10-22):** Use the road matrix derived from coordinates to reorder uncompleted stops, minimizing a bounded combination of kilometres, travel time, and the sum of parcel completion times. Five drop-offs in one area should normally be grouped before one in the opposite area; a newly accepted nearby stop joins the useful cluster. A pickup must precede its own drop-off, and a stop already announced as the rider's next stop remains first to avoid reversing the rider mid-trip. Do not use delivery deadlines or an AI model to choose the sequence. The worker optimizes at most nine upcoming stops per road-matrix call; any later stops retain their current order until they enter that horizon. This is a cost-bounded heuristic, not proof of a globally shortest route.

**Planned direction from carried parcels (2026-10-22):** Determine route direction from the rider's current coordinate to the drop-off coordinates of parcels already in their custody, in committed stop order. A new pickup and drop-off count as aligned only when they advance along one of those directed segments and the road matrix confirms a low-detour insertion. A parcel still awaiting pickup is not treated as goods already in hand. This is planned route direction, not a claim about the phone's measured heading; GPS supplies the current position and freshness. A 200 m tolerance accommodates small coordinate errors, while road travel time remains the feasibility check.

**Company closing time and route grouping (2026-10-22):** Keep matching a waiting local request until a rider with a feasible route becomes available; do not wait for an arbitrary one-hour gap or require a group of three parcels before offering a workable pickup. Group compatible destinations through the fuel-first route planner, up to the six-parcel limit. Use the latest closing time registered for a company's vehicles by default; the company owner may set today's actual closing time (including an overnight close). A legacy company with no registered vehicle hours must set today's close before it receives new local bid invitations. Offer a new rider match only when the estimated complete route finishes at least 20 minutes before that closing time. If road timing is unavailable, keep the request searching rather than assuming it fits. Show an explicitly rough estimate of additional 45-minute rides across online riders after current planned stops. Existing accepted work remains assigned if the owner changes closing time.

**Pickup community selection:** Choose a mapped community such as Eliozu before searching for its streets. When Geoapify supplies a boundary ID, street autocomplete and lookup are restricted to that boundary. Mapping may omit streets or communities; the customer can still correct a location on the map. A street name alone is not treated as an exact building entrance.
**Drop-off community selection:** Use the same community, street, nearby place, and optional pin correction for the delivery location. A Parcel Pal's saved destination seeds its street and pin. The customer must still confirm the recipient agreed to the delivery point; changing the community or street invalidates a previously confirmed pin.
**Delivery request flow:** Show Pickup, Drop-off, and Parcel as separate steps; later steps remain hidden until the current step's required fields and confirmed location are complete. Keep earlier entries when moving back. After the customer selects a community suggestion, show street suggestions; selecting a street or nearby place immediately supplies an approximate point that the customer must confirm. The map stays available as an optional correction, and manual lookup remains a fallback when suggestions are missing. Show a prominent search-in-progress state so customers know to wait for suggestions.

**Two-minute bank-transfer direction (2026-10-19):** Use Flutterwave dynamic NGN virtual accounts with a requested 120-second lifetime for the selected company's payment step. Keep the rider's pickup task read-only until a verified payment and confirmed award. The account and booking hold must be tracked server-side with a unique reference; verify webhook signatures and the transaction amount, currency, status, and reference before releasing the job. Bank-transfer confirmation can arrive after the customer-facing timer, so test that case and reconcile any collected transfer rather than silently discarding it. Do not present an account number or start collecting money until the payable-price rule and Flutterwave sandbox credentials are configured.

**Sender pickup handoff (2026-10-20):** Each job has an eight-digit pickup code shown to its own customer after assignment and confirmed payment. The customer shares it with the assigned rider only when handing over the parcel. The rider enters it in the pickup task; the database verifies it and atomically marks the job picked up. Direct manual status updates cannot bypass this handoff. Five incorrect attempts pause verification for 15 minutes.

**Company operations delegation (2026-10-22):** A logistics company owner can create password-only operations staff and separately permit bidding, rider management, and withdrawals. Staff use a private link and one cryptographically bound phone browser and one bound laptop browser. The owner can edit permissions, reset the password and registered devices, or remove staff. Any permission change invalidates existing staff sessions; revoked or removed staff cannot use company RPCs. Four failed password or device attempts revoke the password and alert the owner. Staff see the same three company work areas with unpermitted sections locked; only owners can create or manage operations staff. Browser device classification is a usability label, not hardware attestation.
