# Port Harcourt hub handoff

Deliveries arriving from another city use one customer order and one billed job. Gohezoh staff record the parcel scan, condition, receiving warehouse and custody time. Operations then assigns a numbered last-mile leg to an active partner approved for Port Harcourt local delivery. The inbound partner assignment is completed, and the new assignment and handoff are audited. The recipient OTP is issued only after the last-mile partner accepts and the job reaches out-for-delivery; the same job is marked delivered once the final OTP is verified.

Before the customer quote is sent, Operations must ensure the last-mile transport leg has an accepted partner quote and is included in the customer-facing quote, without charging the same leg twice. Under the current transport policy, Gohezo withholds 20% of each eligible, completed, collected driver/transport leg from that partner's settlement; the customer's quote does not receive an extra 20% platform-fee line. If the last-mile cost is unknown, hold the quote for review rather than inventing or silently adding a price. For Port Harcourt-bound goods, hub handling and last-mile fees (if any) must be disclosed and accepted separately. This replaces the former 25%-contribution `/ 0.75` driver-transport pricing instruction; see [RENOVATION_SPEC.md](RENOVATION_SPEC.md).

The last-mile leg has a `PHL-` reference for internal custody tracking. It is not a second customer order, customer charge or independent delivery job.
